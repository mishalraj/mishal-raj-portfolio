02 journalctl -u career

newest first · expand a unit for the postmortem

  1. Hotels, but the search has to feel instant and the booking cannot flake. I am here for the unglamorous architecture that makes that true.

    • Driving the migration of the Hotels Search Platform from MWeb to native Android and iOS — same discovery, fewer “please rotate your phone and also your life choices” moments.
    • Backend architecture for Search and Booking, sized for millions of search requests a day. Throughput is a product feature. Latency is a personality test.
    • Designing and tuning highly scalable, low-latency services for performance, reliability, and availability. The boring words that keep the app in the store.

  2. Tried running a startup. It showed up on national television. Pukaar is an AI parenting companion for the first 1,000 days — doctor-verified guidance, cry insights, a calmer 2 a.m. than the alternative.

    • Owned the technology side: product that had to be trustworthy in a domain where “wrong” is not a 500, it is a parent at 2 a.m.
    • Shark Tank India Season 5. Built from a real problem, not a pitch deck that discovered the problem later. Still pretending. The pretending shipped.

    pukaarai.com · Antler · the episode your relatives forwarded on WhatsApp

  3. Payments-as-a-Platform for GrabFin. The brief was simple to say and annoying to do: one interface, many countries, fewer “which rail is this again” meetings.

    • Built a Payments SDK platform that abstracts regional payment integrations into a single contract, so teams can onboard without becoming experts in every local scheme.
    • Unified the messy middle — diverse country integrations, one platform surface. Abstraction: the original coping mechanism, except this one compiled.

  4. This is the long chapter. Offline payments, POS, soundboxes, the physical world trying to talk to a ledger. I led migrations, wrote the services, and stayed close to the devices that actually sit on a merchant counter.

    Lead SDE Nov 2024–May 2025 · Senior SDE Jun 2023–Nov 2024 · Software Engineer Feb 2021–Jun 2023

    • PushToPay migration. Designed and led moving a core offline product onto a unified single-stack, folding online and offline order systems so merchants were not operating two universes. Zero-downtime, because downtime is how you meet new executives.
    • Push to Pay (P2P). Wireless transfer from billing system to POS. New microservice in reactive Java. Scaled to 700,000+ devices. If you have ever pushed a change that far, you understand a certain kind of spirituality.
    • Dynamic QR Soundbox. UPI, wired into billing / POS. Customer does not type the amount. Instant refunds when a QR expires after the account is already debited. Reconciliation that actually reconciles. Fraud has a worse afternoon.
    • Notifications, for real. Replaced FCM with MQTT on ~700,000 Ezetap devices so “notification” meant now, not eventually.
    • Device reliability. Battery diagnostics for 20,000 merchants — the unsexy loop that turns “device is sad” into “device is diagnosable.”
    • Audit trail. Captured executed database queries into MongoDB and built a dashboard on top so business users could see transaction flow and engineers could stop grepping their way through the small fires.
    • Observability. Time-series monitoring on the communications exchange with Influx — collect, graph, notice the cliff before the merchant does.
    • Mentoring, code review, design for new features. The work that does not fit in a bullet but shows up in everyone else’s.

  5. Five months. Java. Git. Everyone’s origin story includes a repo named something like final_final_v2. This was the boot sequence.
